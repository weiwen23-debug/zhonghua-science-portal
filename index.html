💧 Float or Sink?

Investigate what happens when different objects are placed in water.

Science challenge: Investigate seven familiar materials: metal, plastic, glass, rubber, wood, fabric and ceramic. These outcomes describe the specific objects shown, not every object made from each material. Object shape, trapped air and water absorption can change what happens. This is a simplified simulation; try real objects with a teacher too.

1. Choose an object

Choose one object from each of the seven materials. Tap or drag it into the water tank.

Change the test object

Investigate shape and size. Keep the material the same when comparing.

2. Make a prediction

Choose an object first.

Predict before testing, just like a scientist.

3. Observe the experiment

Select an object and make a prediction.

Fair-test challenge: Compare a small and large wooden block. Does changing size alone change whether it floats? Then compare a solid metal ball and a hollow metal boat. What changed besides size?

6. Draw a conclusion

Compare the seven materials, and compare different forms or sizes of the same material. Which tested objects floated or sank? Use evidence from your results. Can you conclude that all objects made from a material will behave the same way?
