💧 Float or Sink?

Investigate what happens when spoons made of different materials are placed in water.

Science challenge: Compare spoons made from metal, plastic, glass, rubber, wood, fabric and ceramic. The fabric sample is a spoon-shaped cotton cutout, not a rigid usable spoon. Results are illustrative for these particular samples: plastic and rubber vary by composition, and fabric may float briefly before becoming wet. Shape, trapped air and water absorption also affect floating. Test real samples with your teacher to check these predictions.

1. Choose an object

Choose a spoon made from each of the seven materials. All spoon illustrations have the same size and shape. Tap a spoon card below to select it, then make a prediction. Dragging is optional.
